## Problem

The lede is four lines and ends on its strongest clause:

> Spaces get named after directories, because that is all herdr knew when you
> opened them. The agent inside each one has been describing its own work the
> entire time. namesync moves that name onto the space, keeps it current as the
> work changes, and leaves the ones you named yourself alone.

"Leaves the ones you named yourself alone" is the promise that separates this
from every other renamer, and it is the twenty-eighth word of a sentence that
has already made three claims. Anyone who stops reading at the mock never gets
to it.

The headline is not the problem and should not be touched -- the italic turn is
the best thing on the page.

## Proposal

Shorter, and the held-names promise no longer last. The mock underneath already
shows two held rows, so the lede and the picture would be making the same point
at the same moment instead of three lines apart.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Shorter than the current four lines
- [ ] The held-names promise is not the final clause
- [ ] The headline is unchanged
