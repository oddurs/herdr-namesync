The page currently runs: hero, "Nothing generates the name, unless you ask it
to", "Why another renamer", "When it renames", "Install".

The second section is a how-it-works claim and the third is the why-should-I-
care claim. A visitor is asked to take an architectural position on name
generation before being given a reason to care that this plugin exists at all.

Swap them. "Why another renamer" answers the question a reader arrives with --
herdr's marketplace lists twenty-three of these -- and "Nothing generates the
name" is much stronger as the answer to *how*, once the *why* has landed.

Also drop the last paragraph of "Nothing generates the name": "So the
complexity goes somewhere other than generation: into deciding when a name
should change." The section that follows it demonstrates exactly that, and
asserting it first spends the surprise.

Pure reordering, no copy to write. Do it before the comparison table lands, so
the table is written into its final position.

Done when: "Why another renamer" is the first section after the hero, and the
trailing paragraph is gone.
