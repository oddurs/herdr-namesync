Three new sections are proposed -- one title becoming two names, what the
sidebar actually gets, and what namesync will not do. Adding all three takes
the page from five sections to eight, and a landing page that argues for eight
screens stops being a landing page.

Two of the three can fold instead of standing alone:

- **"Two surfaces, two shapes"** can become a seventh row in the `rules` list
  rather than its own section, at the cost of the two-column visual.
- **"What the sidebar gets"** can become an annotation on the hero mock, which
  is arguably where it belongs anyway -- the mock is already showing `$project`,
  `worktree`, `held` and a duration, and the explanation is currently nowhere.

"What it will not do" does not fold. It is the trust section and it needs its
own quiet.

So the real choice is eight sections with a clean rhythm, or six with two ideas
carried by existing furniture. This blocks 0047 and 0048, which is why it is
its own item rather than a paragraph in one of them.

Worth noting the rhythm argument on the other side: the page today is one
visual and four screens of prose, and each new section is proposed with a
distinct visual form -- table, definition list, two-column diagram, quiet
prose, code. Length is not the only variable.

Done when: the arc is decided and 0047 and 0048 say which form they are taking.
